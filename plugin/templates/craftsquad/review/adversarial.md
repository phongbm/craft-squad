### Lens: Adversarial Review
Assume the change is wrong until the diff proves otherwise. Look for what the author did not think about, not what they did.
- Does the diff do exactly what the Story/Epic OUTPUT lists, no more and no less?
- Which promised acceptance criterion has no code or test behind it?
- What breaks for the next caller, the next migration, the next concurrent request?
- A review with zero findings is suspicious: re-read the riskiest hunk before concluding PASS.
